## Final generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a completely new logo for Phone 3D Studio, a sophisticated Mac application for spatial interaction. The previous literal phone-and-ball illustrations were rejected. This design must take an entirely different direction.
Design one extremely clean, distinctive abstract geometric brand mark: a single substantial folded ribbon that forms a compact open rectangular portal and subtly suggests a capital P through its negative space. One precise change of plane makes a flat window feel spatial. The mark should feel like a considered identity for a professional creative tool, not game artwork, an emoji, or an object render.
Style: graphic-design precision, flat vector-like geometry, bold simple silhouette, carefully balanced negative space, lightly softened corners. At most two broad flat color planes: deep teal and fresh mint-green, creating a restrained suggestion of dimension. No gradients, glossy highlights, textures or realistic lighting. The cleverness must come from geometry.
Composition: centered standalone symbol at roughly 70 percent of a square 1024x1024 canvas; ample negative space; designed to read at 24 pixels. Genuine transparent alpha background. Deliver the symbol alone.
Do not depict a smartphone, tablet, ball, marble, screen hardware, arrows, orbit, cube illustration, infinity loop, or mascot. No outer rounded-square app tile, no floor or shadow. No wordmark, written text, label, comparison board, watermark or presentation mockup. Avoid generic tangled ribbons; the silhouette must be simple enough to sketch with a few deliberate strokes. Crisp clean antialiased edges. One finished logo.
